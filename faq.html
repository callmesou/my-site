The basics

  • It's a reusable furnace filter. A durable metal frame with a washable cloth inside, designed to replace the disposable cardboard filter you currently buy every 2–3 months.
  • A typical home spends $300–$500+ on disposable filters over 10 years. Filter4Life is a one-time $199 purchase. After the first 2–3 years, everything you'd have spent on disposables stays in your wallet.
  • Yes. Filter4Life is independently tested and rated MERV-8 — the same rating as the standard disposable filter you'd buy at the hardware store. It captures dust, pollen, pet hair, lint, and common household allergens.

Fit & sizing

  • Pull out your current filter. The size is printed right on the cardboard edge (e.g., "20 × 20 × 1"). The first two numbers are width and height — that's all you need to match. See our sizing guide for details.
  • Not yet. These three cover the majority of Canadian homes. We're expanding our size catalog — if yours isn't listed, let us know and we'll notify you when it's available.
  • Our current filters are the standard 1-inch depth that fits most residential furnaces. Thicker filter slots (common in higher-end HVAC systems) will need an adapter or a different product. Contact us before ordering if you're unsure.
  • Filter4Life is designed specifically for furnace / forced-air heating systems at the standard 1-inch depth. We don't currently support portable air purifiers or through-wall AC units.

Installation & use

  • It's the exact same process as changing a disposable filter. Pull the old one out, slide the new one in, make sure the airflow arrow on the frame points the same direction as your old filter's arrow. About 60 seconds, no tools.
  • Good practice says yes — turn your furnace off at the thermostat before removing any filter. It's quick (a couple of seconds) and protects the system.
  • Every 1–3 months is the sweet spot for most homes. Homes with pets, smokers, or high furnace use might check monthly. A quick visual inspection takes seconds.

Care & cleaning

  • Toss it in with your regular laundry. Cold or warm water, any standard detergent, tumble dry or air dry. No special cycle, no dry cleaning.
  • Yes. Tumble dry on low or medium. If you prefer to be extra gentle, you can also air dry — it takes about 6–8 hours to fully dry.
  • Each cloth insert is designed for 3–5 years of normal use. Since every kit comes with two, most homes get 5+ years before needing replacement cloths.

Product details

  • Powder-coated steel. Built to handle repeated use, temperature shifts, and a decade of airflow without warping or corroding.
  • Washable microfiber, rated MERV-8 for particulate capture. Designed to survive dozens of laundry cycles without losing filtration effectiveness.
  • Yes. The frame is manufactured in Canada, and our cloth inserts are sourced and finished domestically. Customer support is also Canada-based.

Orders & shipping

  • Free, anywhere in Canada. No minimum order.
  • Orders ship within 1–2 business days. Delivery is typically 3–7 business days depending on where you are in Canada. See our shipping page for full details.
  • Not at this time. We're Canada-only for now.

Warranty & returns

  • If your metal frame fails under normal use within 10 years, we replace it free. That covers warping, rust, structural defects — any real manufacturing issue. See our guarantee page for the full terms.
  • Yes. 30-day returns, free shipping both ways. We refund you in full, no questions asked.
  • The cloth is a consumable — designed for 3–5 years per insert. Replacement 2-packs will be available soon at a fraction of the original kit cost.
