— 30-second sizing guide

Find your filter size.

Don't worry — you don't need a measuring tape. The size you need is almost always printed right on your current filter. Here's how to find it.

01

Find your furnace.

It's usually in the basement, a utility closet, or the garage. Look for a large metal box (often grey, beige, or white) with a vent pipe going up into the ceiling.

If you have a condo or apartment, the furnace or air handler might be in a dedicated mechanical closet.

02

Turn it off.

Just for a minute. Go to your thermostat and switch it to "Off". This stops the fan while you're working.

03

Find the filter slot.

On your furnace, look for a flat rectangular slot on the side — usually close to the bottom, sometimes covered by a small metal door or panel. Pop it open.

Slide out the current filter. It'll look like a thin rectangle made of cardboard with pleated material inside.

04

Read the size on the edge.

Look at the cardboard edge of your old filter. The size is printed right there, usually in a format like this:

— Example
20 × 20 × 1
The first two numbers are width and height in inches. The third is depth (usually 1").

If you see 20 × 20, 20 × 16, or 20 × 25, we have a kit that fits you directly. The order of the first two numbers doesn't matter — 16 × 20 is the same as 20 × 16.

05

That's it. Order your kit.

Match your size to the right product below. Not sure? Send us a photo of your current filter's edge and we'll confirm it for you.
