— The promise

Guaranteed for ten years.

We stand behind every frame we ship with a full 10-year guarantee. If it fails under normal use, we replace it. No fine print, no proof of purchase hunting, no hassle.

10
Years

If it breaks, we replace it.

Every Filter4Life frame is built to outlast a decade of use. If something goes wrong — and it shouldn't — we'll send you a new one. That's the whole guarantee.

What's covered

The 10-year guarantee covers the metal frame of every Filter4Life kit. Specifically, if you experience any of the following under normal residential use, we'll replace the frame at no cost:

  • Structural failure — cracking, bending, or breakage that affects fit or function
  • Rust or corrosion — unexpected deterioration of the frame material
  • Manufacturing defects — anything that was wrong from the moment it left our warehouse
  • Premature warping — if the frame loses its shape and no longer seats properly

What's not covered

A few common-sense exclusions apply:

  • Intentional damage or misuse — dropping, bending, crushing, or cutting the frame
  • Non-residential heavy use — industrial environments, commercial HVAC, or conditions outside typical home use
  • Cloth insert wear — the washable cloth is a consumable and has its own lifespan (see below)
  • Cosmetic issues — minor scratches or discoloration from normal use that don't affect function
  • Filters modified from their original form — painting, cutting, or retrofitting voids the guarantee

About the cloth inserts

Each Filter4Life kit comes with two washable cloth inserts. These are the filtering component that catches dust and pollen, and they're designed for 3–5 years of normal use each. Since you get two per kit, most homes get 5+ years before needing to think about replacements.

Cloth inserts are consumables, not covered under the 10-year guarantee. If a cloth fails significantly earlier than expected (within the first year of normal use), contact us — we'll make it right.

Replacement cloth 2-packs will be available for purchase at a fraction of the full kit cost.

How to claim your guarantee

It's simple. Send an email to hello@filter4life.ca with:

  1. A brief description of the problem
  2. A photo or two showing the issue
  3. Your approximate purchase date or order number (if you have it — don't worry if you don't)

We'll review your claim within 1–2 business days. If it's covered, we'll ship you a replacement frame at no cost, including shipping. You don't need to return the damaged one in most cases.

No receipt? No problem.

We understand that paperwork disappears over a decade. If you ordered from us, we have a record. Just let us know the approximate purchase date and the email address you used to order — we'll look it up.

Why we offer this

We designed Filter4Life to last at least ten years. If we got that wrong for your specific frame, that's our problem to solve, not yours.

A 10-year guarantee isn't a marketing gimmick — it's an honest expression of how long we expect the product to work. If it doesn't, we want to know, and we want to fix it.
