— The complete guide

Easier than changing a lightbulb.

If you've ever done laundry, you already know how to use this. Here's exactly how to install Filter4Life, keep it clean, and enjoy fresh air for years without ever buying another filter.

01

Install it.

Find your furnace — usually in the basement, a utility closet, or garage. Look for the flat rectangular slot on the side of the unit. Pull out your old disposable filter and slide the Filter4Life frame in its place.

Your old filter had an arrow printed on its edge showing airflow direction. Make sure Filter4Life's arrow points the same way.

⏱ 60 seconds 🔧 No tools ✅ First-timer friendly
02

Check it every 1–3 months.

Peek at the cloth insert every month or two. When it looks grey or fuzzy with dust, it's time for a swap. Homes with pets, smokers, or heavy furnace use might need to check more often.

Pull the dirty cloth out and slide the spare clean one in (every kit comes with two). That's it — fresh filtration in 30 seconds, no tools.

⏱ 30 seconds 👀 Visual check 🔄 Swap the spare
03

Wash the dirty one.

Toss the dirty cloth into your regular laundry — no special detergent, no separate cycle, no dry cleaning. Use cold or warm water. Tumble dry or air dry.

Once it's clean, store it somewhere dry until your next swap. That's the whole maintenance routine. You'll do it 4–6 times a year and forget about it the rest of the time.

🧺 Regular laundry 💧 Any water temp ♻️ Reuse indefinitely
— Pro tips

Small things that make it even easier.

Set a calendar reminder

Add a phone reminder for the first Saturday of every season. Fall, winter, spring, summer — check, swap, wash. Done.

Wash with towels

The cloth is lint-light, so it pairs perfectly with a towel load. Same temperature, same cycle, zero extra effort.

Air dry if unsure

Tumble dry works fine, but if you want to be extra gentle, hang it overnight. It'll be ready to swap in by morning.

Keep one in a drawer

Store the spare cloth somewhere easy to find — a utility drawer, the shelf above your laundry machine. You'll swap in seconds.

Vacuum it between washes

For a quick refresh between laundry days, run a handheld vacuum over the surface. Removes 80% of the dust in 10 seconds.

Track your savings

Every time you'd have bought a disposable filter, mark it on a sticky note. Watch the savings pile up month after month.
