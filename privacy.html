Privacy policy.

A straightforward explanation of what information we collect, what we do with it, and how we protect it. No dense legalese, no hidden clauses.

This is a plain-English summary of our privacy practices. This document is provided as a starting framework — before going live, Filter4Life should have this reviewed by a Canadian privacy lawyer to ensure compliance with PIPEDA and applicable provincial privacy laws (like Quebec's Law 25).

1. Who we are

Filter4Life is a Canadian company selling reusable furnace filters. When you shop with us, sign up for updates, or contact us, we collect some personal information to provide our services.

2. What we collect

We collect only what we need to run our business. Specifically:

  • Order information: name, shipping address, billing address, email, phone number, and the items you purchased
  • Payment information: processed securely through our payment provider — we do not store your full credit card number on our systems
  • Support correspondence: messages, photos, and details you send us through the contact form or by email
  • Website analytics: basic visit data (pages viewed, referring site, device type, approximate location) to help us improve the site

3. How we use it

We use your information to:

  • Process and ship your orders
  • Provide customer support
  • Honor your 10-year guarantee
  • Send you occasional product updates (only if you opt in)
  • Improve our website and products
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations

We do not sell your information to third parties. Ever.

4. Who we share it with

We share limited information with trusted service providers who help us operate:

  • Shopify — our e-commerce platform
  • Payment processors — to securely handle transactions
  • Canada Post and shipping carriers — to deliver your order
  • Email service providers — to send order confirmations and updates
  • Analytics providers — to measure site performance (data is aggregated)

All of these providers are required to protect your data and use it only for the purposes we specify.

5. Cookies

Our website uses cookies for basic functionality (keeping you logged in, remembering your cart) and for analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some site features may not work properly without them.

6. Your rights

Under Canadian privacy law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we have about you
  • Correct any inaccuracies
  • Delete your information (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@filter4life.ca. We'll respond within 30 days.

7. How we protect your data

We use industry-standard security practices: encrypted connections (SSL/TLS), secure payment processing through PCI-compliant providers, and limited internal access to customer data.

No system is 100% secure, but we take data protection seriously and will notify you promptly if a breach occurs that affects your personal information.

8. How long we keep your data

We keep order records as long as required for tax and warranty purposes (typically 7–10 years). Marketing preferences and account data are kept until you request deletion or your account is inactive for a prolonged period.

9. Children

Our products and website are not intended for children under 16. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes to this policy, we'll post the updated version here and update the "last updated" date at the top. For material changes, we'll email customers who have given us their email address.

11. Contact us

Questions, concerns, or requests about privacy? Email privacy@filter4life.ca or use the contact form.
