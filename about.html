— Our story

We got tired of throwing money in the trash.

Every 2–3 months, a nearly-identical cardboard-and-fiberglass rectangle ends up in a Canadian landfill. We thought: what if it didn't have to?

One filter. Built to last a decade.

Filter4Life started with a simple frustration. The average Canadian home spends hundreds of dollars a decade on disposable furnace filters — and throws every single one of them away.

The industry has stayed the same for decades: thin cardboard frames, fiberglass pleats, 60 days of life, into the trash. No innovation. No thought about where it goes when you're done.

We asked: what if you bought one sturdy metal frame, put a washable cloth inside, and kept it for 10+ years? No quarterly orders. No landfill waste. No reinventing your routine.

That's Filter4Life. One product. Made in Canada. Guaranteed for a decade.

— Made in Canada

Built here. Supported here.

Every Filter4Life frame is manufactured in Canada. The cloth inserts are sourced and finished domestically. When you buy from us, your dollars circulate through Canadian workshops, suppliers, and shipping partners.

That matters because it means faster shipping, local accountability, and a real person answering your emails if something goes wrong. No international supply-chain mysteries. No six-week lead times.
