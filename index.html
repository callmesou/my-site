— In plain words

It's a filter you wash instead of throw away.

A furnace filter is the thin rectangle that slides into your furnace or heating system. It traps dust, pet hair, and pollen so your air stays cleaner. Most people buy a new one every 2–3 months and throw the old one in the trash.

Our filter is a sturdy metal frame with a washable cloth inside. When it gets dirty, pull the cloth out, toss it in your laundry, and put it back. Same clean air — no more buying filters.

— How it works

Three simple steps. Then you're done.

If you've ever done laundry, you already know how to use this.

01

Slide it in

Pull out your old disposable filter. Slide Filter4Life into the same spot. Arrow points in the direction of airflow. No tools.

Takes 60 seconds
02

Check it every 1–3 months

When the cloth looks dirty, pull it out. Swap in the spare clean one (each kit comes with two). Your air keeps flowing.

Takes 30 seconds
03

Toss it in the wash

The dirty cloth goes in your regular laundry. No special detergent. Dry it, and it's ready for next time. Keep doing this for years.
