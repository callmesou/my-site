Shipping & returns.

Free shipping anywhere in Canada. 30-day returns, free both ways, no questions asked. Here are the details if you want to know more.

Free Canada shipping

Every order, every size, no minimum. Ships anywhere in Canada.

Ships in 1–2 days

Orders placed on business days leave our warehouse within 48 hours.

30-day easy returns

Doesn't fit? Doesn't work for you? Full refund, free return shipping.

Shipping

Where we ship

Filter4Life ships anywhere in Canada — from the territories to the Maritimes. At this time, we do not ship internationally or to the United States.

How much it costs

Shipping is always free, on every order, regardless of size or quantity. No minimum purchase, no promo code needed.

How long it takes

Orders placed Monday through Friday ship within 1–2 business days. Once shipped, typical delivery times are:

  • Southern Ontario, Quebec, Montreal area: 2–4 business days
  • Rest of Canada (major cities): 3–6 business days
  • Remote and northern areas: 5–10 business days

You'll receive a tracking number by email as soon as your order leaves our warehouse.

Order confirmation

You'll get a confirmation email immediately after placing your order, and a separate shipping notification when it goes out. If you don't see them, check your spam folder or contact us.

Order changes

Need to change your size, address, or cancel your order? Email hello@filter4life.ca within 24 hours of ordering and we'll do our best to accommodate. Once the order has shipped, any changes become a return (see below).

Returns

30-day return window

You have 30 days from the date of delivery to request a return for any reason. That's plenty of time to install the filter, make sure it fits, and decide whether to keep it.

What qualifies

We accept returns on Filter4Life kits that are:

  • Unused or lightly used (installed and tested is fine)
  • In their original packaging, or equivalent
  • Within the 30-day return window

If your kit is significantly damaged beyond normal installation, we'll work with you on a partial refund or exchange.

How to start a return

Email hello@filter4life.ca with your order number and a quick reason. We'll send you a pre-paid return shipping label. Drop the package at any Canada Post location — return shipping is on us.

Refund timing

Once we receive your return, we inspect it and issue your refund to the original payment method within 5–7 business days. Depending on your bank or credit card, the refund may take an additional 2–5 days to appear.

Exchanges

If you ordered the wrong size, we'll happily exchange it for the right one. Same process: email us, we send a label, you ship back, we ship out the correct size as soon as we receive the return.

Damaged or wrong items

If your Filter4Life arrives damaged or we sent you the wrong size, let us know within 7 days of delivery. Send us a photo and your order number to hello@filter4life.ca. We'll send a replacement right away and arrange for the original to be returned — no extra steps or fees on your side.

Questions?

If any of the above wasn't clear, or your situation doesn't quite fit, just email us. We're a small Canadian team and we read every message. Get in touch.
